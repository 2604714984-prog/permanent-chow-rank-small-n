\begin{abstract}
We prove over every field of characteristic zero that
\[
  \ChowRank(\perm_4)=8.
\]
The lower bound uses the second-derivative space, a classification of its
low-rank quadrics, a two-quotient rank inequality, and an exact rational chart
minor. The upper bound is Glynn's eight-term decomposition. Independent
programs reconstruct every computation used in the proof.
\end{abstract}
