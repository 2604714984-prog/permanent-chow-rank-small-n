## perm5

Title: The Chow Rank of the Five-by-Five Permanent

Author: Rongyu Xu

Abstract:

We prove that the ordinary Chow rank of the permanent of a generic five by
five matrix is sixteen over every field of characteristic zero. The lower
bound uses an image-span inequality for rectangular catalecticants and a
quotient-symbol estimate for a single quintic product. The finite verification
records a 352-case squarefree table, four small rational derivative matrices,
and elementary partition bounds. Their inputs and independent verification
programs are included. The proof makes no assertion about border Chow rank.

MSC 2020: Primary 15A69; Secondary 14N05, 68V05.

## perm6

Title: The Chow Rank of the Six-by-Six Permanent

Author: Rongyu Xu

Abstract:

Let $\operatorname{perm}_6$ be the permanent of a generic $6$ by $6$ matrix.
Over every field of characteristic zero, we prove that its ordinary Chow
rank is $32$. The finite computations are stated as explicit lemmas with
exhaustive domains and exact outputs. The lower bound combines a symmetric
image-span inequality with a half-defect estimate for arbitrary quotient
derivative symbols of a sextic Chow term. An exact verification reconstructs
all finite tables. A second implementation independently checks every
labelled bipartite graph with at most six edges and all $45,696$ coordinate
squarefree-symbol cases. No floating-point or randomized step is used.
A separate standard-library implementation independently expands and
differentiates all five dependent-factor normal forms. No border-rank or
general-$n$ equality is claimed.

MSC 2020: Primary 15A69; Secondary 14N05, 68V05.
